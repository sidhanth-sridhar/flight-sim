NEXT TASK 1 — THE TRIM TAB MUST RIDE THE ELEVATOR
====================================================================
The pilot's report: moving the elevator up and down leaves a **small piece on the right
elevator that does not move** — it just sits there.

**Diagnosis (already done — verify it, then fix it):** the `TrimTab` decoration
(`Cessna172.luau` ~line 877) is a separate hinged part on the right elevator, but
`AircraftBuilder.build` gives **every** hinged surface a Motor6D with **`Part0 = root`**
(`AircraftBuilder.luau` ~line 374). So the tab is anchored to the airframe and only rotates
on its own `trim` channel — it never follows the elevator's deflection. On a real 172 the
tab is hinged to the elevator's trailing edge, so it moves **with** the elevator **and** adds
its own trim deflection on top (compound motion).

**The fix:** support an optional `hinge.parent` in the builder — when present, the trim tab's
Motor6D gets `Part0 = parts[hinge.parent]` (the right elevator part) and its `C0` is computed
relative to that part's rest position instead of the root's. Set `TrimTab.hinge.parent =
"ElevatorRight"` in the definition. `SurfaceAnimation` needs no channel change — it already
drives the `trim` channel — but its `baseC0 * rotation` rest-motion assumption still holds
because the tab's own hinge C0 is now in the elevator's frame.

**Constraints that must stay green (do not "simplify" past them):**
- The tab must STILL deflect on trim, opposing the elevator it trims — `SurfaceAnimation.runTests`
  pins this (`"The trim tab opposes the elevator it trims"`). Deleting the hinge or welding the
  tab to the elevator kills it. The tab needs BOTH motions: ride the elevator + own trim.
- `"Everything welds into ONE assembly"` in `AircraftBuilder.runTests` — a chained Motor6D keeps
  the tab in the assembly (it already is today via its motor).
- `Cessna172` suite (33 checks), `SurfaceAnimation` suite, `AircraftBuilder` suite must all stay
  green; the Controls contract (six fields) is untouched.
- The mirroring test reads `ElevatorRight`/`ElevatorLeft` from the definition — the trim tab is
  right-elevator-only by design (the comment at `Cessna172.luau` ~line 871), do not pair it.
