====================================================================

the pan is 1.40 m above the wheels where a real 172's is about 0.95 m, so the seat is roughly
0.4 m too high in a cabin that is otherwise correctly sized. The eye is already near the top of
the 0.155 m slot between the glareshield (y 0.695) and the wing underside (y 0.850).
