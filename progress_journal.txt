2026-08-03 (Mon) — Day 1: the simulator's core is built

Issues faced:
- Centre-of-mass frame mismatch: Roblox reports AssemblyCenterOfMass from the
  root part, the definition measures from a datum — fixed with a DatumOffset

  came out 21% light — fixed with a SHAPE_VOLUME_FACTOR table.

  a subtree whose root it did not create. Fixed by deleting the folder in
  Studio and letting Rojo recreate it.
- Windmilling RPM read negative on a parked aircraft (forward airspeed drifts
  negative) — clamped at zero.

991/991 across 24 suites (was 881). +110 checks, two new suites.

BUILT (6 of 8 items complete, 3 half)
1. Rudder trim tab - DONE. hinge.parent = "Rudder" so it rides AND trims,
   own channel, own keys, compound-motion test on a real built model.
2. Fuel selector - DONE. Two tanks, OFF/LEFT/BOTH/RIGHT, line fuel, real
   starvation. BOTH makes up a shortfall from either side, which is WHY it is
   the takeoff position; LEFT on an empty tank starves with the other full.
3. Mixture - DONE. Correct mixture IS the density ratio. Fuel flow scales
   with lean ratio and power does not - that asymmetry is why leaning is
   worth doing. Idle cutoff stops the engine; the throttle cannot.
4. Magnetos - DONE. OFF-R-L-BOTH, held starter, mag check against the POH's
   150 RPM / 50 RPM differential limits. The factor reaches RPM as well as
   power, because the drop is what the pilot READS.
5. Electrical - DONE. New module. 28 V / 24 V / 60 A / 13 Ah, all published.
   Ammeter shows rate of charge. Alternator cannot self-excite with BAT off,
   which is why the checklist does BAT before ALT.
6. Engine gauges - HALF. Oil pressure, oil temp, fuel flow MODELLED and in
   the POH bands. NOT DRAWN - no SixPack dials yet.
7. Cabin environment - HALF. State + keys + electrical load. No 3D mirror.
8. Lights - HALF. State + keys + bus load. The parts do not illuminate yet.
+  Binds reference (K) - DONE. 42 bindings, read from DEFAULT_BINDINGS, not
   a copy. Absorbs nothing (counted, not trusted). Verified with a real

The three halves are all the same missing piece: the VISUAL layer. Next task

THE BRIEF'S PREMISE ABOUT THE FUEL SPLIT WAS WRONG, AND MEASURING IT IS THE
FINDING
- Expected: two tanks move the CoM, re-verify section 4.
- Measured: they move NOTHING. The fuel is already in the STATIC mass boxes
  (WingLeft/WingRight 110 kg each, "structure plus the fuel it carries") and
  those never change as fuel burns. fuelKg was never a mass.
- So section 4 stands unchanged - verified by a test that reads the two wing
  masses out of the definition, not assumed.
- OPEN DECISION FOR THE PILOT: making an imbalance produce a real rolling
  moment means making the wing boxes DYNAMIC. That is a mass-model change and
  a section 4 decision, deliberately not done here.

THREE BUGS ON CODE THAT LOOKED RIGHT
1. The rudder trim tab FOLLOWED the rudder instead of opposing it - built
   sign = +1 like the rudder. Section 37's lesson on the other axis: the
   opposition lives in the two signs. Now +1/-1.
2. The tab hung past the trailing edge and grew the aeroplane to 15.755 m
   against section 45's 15.500 target. The real tab is INSET; the published-
   length check caught it on the first run.
3. The mixture curve disagreed with its own comment - a plain square gave
   0.87 power at best economy where the note claimed 0.95. Exponent now
   DERIVED from the figure it must hit (2.5).
Plus two in the electrical suite: a test that tripped a breaker by inflating
the loads the breaker limit is DERIVED from (so the limit rose with the fault
and nothing tripped), and a bus voltage computed before the battery drained,
reporting 20.4 V on a battery measured at 0.00 Ah.

THE KEYED START NEEDED ONE LINE AND WAS NEARLY MISSED
engineCommanded is E's latch and the branch below stops the engine when it is
false - so a pilot starting with Y would have had the engine stopped one frame
after it caught. Cranking now sets the commanded state. Both ways in are kept:
E is the habit every existing test depends on, Y is the procedure.

- Every switch keyboard-bound, no clickable knobs (section 7).
